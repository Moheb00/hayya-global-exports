# HAYYA Export Hub

Build a premium, modern, professional bilingual website for an Egyptian agricultural products export company called **HAYYA**.

The website should look like a real international B2B agricultural export company — NOT like a generic template, small local business website, or overly colorful agricultural website.

## BRAND

Company name: **HAYYA**

Use the uploaded HAYYA logo as the main brand identity.

The logo colors should inspire the overall visual identity:

* Deep navy / dark blue
* Warm orange / golden yellow
* Agricultural green
* White / off-white backgrounds

Keep the design elegant, premium, clean, trustworthy, and corporate.

## BUSINESS

HAYYA is an Egyptian company focused on exporting agricultural products and fresh produce.

The company is preparing to export Egyptian agricultural products to international markets.

HAYYA has contractual/business relationships with:

* **Tahya Misr**
* **El Watanya**

These relationships should be presented carefully as strategic/business partnerships or contracted entities, without inventing certifications, government endorsements, awards, licenses, export volumes, countries, or claims that were not provided.

## WEBSITE GOAL

The primary goal is to make international buyers, importers, distributors, wholesalers, and food businesses trust HAYYA and contact the company.

The website should communicate:

**Egyptian agricultural products → professional sourcing → quality control → reliable supply → international export**

The site should feel like a company that can handle serious B2B export inquiries.

## LANGUAGE

Create the website in:

1. English — default language
2. Arabic — secondary language

Add a highly visible language switcher:

**EN | AR**

The Arabic version must be properly RTL.

Do NOT simply translate the interface word-for-word. Make both languages feel professionally written and natural.

# VISUAL DESIGN DIRECTION

The website must feel:

* Premium
* Corporate
* International
* Agricultural
* Trustworthy
* Modern
* Minimal
* B2B
* Export-oriented

Avoid:

* Cartoon illustrations
* Excessive green
* Cheap gradients
* Generic stock-photo layouts
* Huge rounded cards everywhere
* Excessive animations
* Clutter
* Fake statistics
* Fake certifications
* Fake awards
* Fake customer logos

Use:

* Large editorial-style photography
* Strong typography
* Sophisticated spacing
* Deep navy sections
* Warm orange/gold accent from the logo
* Controlled agricultural green accents
* Subtle borders
* Soft shadows
* Elegant hover effects
* Smooth scrolling
* Professional micro-interactions

The design should resemble a premium international food/agricultural trading company rather than a basic farming website.
